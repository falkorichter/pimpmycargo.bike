<p>If the urban arrow is the VW Touran of cargo bikes, this is the Golf. They also make an Pedelec version. Meet the Muli. You can back one now and get a good deal on it <a href="https://www.startnext.com/muli">startnext.com/muli</a>.   My thoughs: </p>
<ul>
<li>rides great</li>
<li>brakes a great</li>
<li>rides like an urban arrow, but lighter </li>
<li>build quality was very good, frame and weld looked very well made </li>
<li>small front wheel was no problem on flat tarmac</li>
<li>price is hefty, but It’s a cargo bike! They are never cheap.</li>
<li>mechanism to open and close the cargo bay was very solid</li>
<li>no rain protection of the cargo</li>
<li>metal mesh used for cargo bay, very solid</li>
</ul>
