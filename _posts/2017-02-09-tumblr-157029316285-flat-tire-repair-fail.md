<h2>Flat tire repair #fail</h2>
<p>I had a flat tire. The non-flatable tires ran flat. Thank you Schwalbe. January 2017, 8pm after a long day of work. 0°C outside temperature.</p>
<p>I tried this tire repair stuff called “Reifen Pilot” as you can see in the picture above.</p>
<p>It was a mess, and didn´t work. Partly because of the cold, I was too stupid to put screw it on the tire vent properly and half of the <strong>goo </strong>was all over the place but not in the tire.</p>
<p>Took off the chain and shifter cables. The cables turned out to to be impossible to put back on.</p>
<p>What a mess. But I was getting there. You can still see the tire repair goo on the tire.</p>
<p>The back brake was not working anyways anymore, so I decided to skip the re-assembly.</p>
<p>It was too cold to take off the pump adapter. And it took the vent with it. What a mess.</p>
