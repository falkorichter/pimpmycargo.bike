<h2>new brakes - planning</h2>
<p>tI was not very happy with the existing brakes on my Urban Arrow. We bought the bike in April 2016  and we might put around 3000km on it in the first year. </p>
<ul>
<li>The front brake pads had to be replaced in December</li>
<li>The front brakes squeaked when using them</li>
<li>The back brake lost oil/power. By January 2017 they literally did not do anything</li>
<li>My bike shop told me the brakes are basically entry level Shimano brakes. </li>
</ul>
<p>So I decided, I want better new brakes. I know that Bullitt cargo bikes by larry vs Harry were very well equipped with Magura MT5 brakes. Read <a href="http://www.larryvsharry.com/magura-mt5-showstopper/">their blog</a> post on the matter.</p>
<p>There was no information to be found on the web about this, so we ordered Magura MT7 brakes (MT5) were out of stock. They don´t fit the frame.</p>
<h3>I decided: I want double caliper brakes with the largest possible discs. Ideally in red :)</h3>
<p>I then decided to call/email Urban Arrow directly and ask them:</p>
<blockquote>Hallo Falko,</blockquote>
<blockquote>First of all thanks for your email and a happy new year. Below you will find an answer to your question:</blockquote>
<blockquote>For the front of the bike there aren’t problems as far as we know most brakes will fit.</blockquote>
<blockquote>For the rear of the bike this is different and it is more difficult to mount other brakes on this frame.</blockquote>
<blockquote>Magura doesn’t fit.</blockquote>
<blockquote>Shimano Single piston: </blockquote>
<blockquote>Deore T615 will fit with 180mm or 203mm disc</blockquote>
<blockquote>Alfine or XT will only fit with 203mm disc</blockquote>
<blockquote>Shimano Double pistons:</blockquote>
<blockquote>Zee or Saint will both fit with 180mm or 203mm disc. But it is more difficult to tighten the top inbus because the calipers are bigger. (But it is possible)</blockquote>
<blockquote>Note: When using 203mm disc i is more difficult to release the rear wheel from the frame for example when you are having a flat tyre.</blockquote>
<blockquote>If you have further questions, don’t hesitate to ask us?</blockquote>
<blockquote>Met vriendelijke groet / Best regards,</blockquote>
